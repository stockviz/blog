## Q1. Did we overfit to a regime?

**Answer: YES — extensively documented, and the central finding of the
program.**

- The only "positive" daily result (R1.5 top-50: book Sharpe +1.21 vs MA
  +1.14) was exposed by the wider-universe follow-up as **drift-betting**,
  not skill: the pnl-reward TDQN learns the training window's best
  constant direction (runs/r15_ff60/findings.md, the 4-attempt collapse
  taxonomy: always-short in a bear slice, always-flat in the long-only
  corner). The train window (2015-19, mega-cap bull) and test window
  (2020+, melt-up) were the SAME regime — regime alignment masqueraded
  as edge.
- Every OOS window across R1.1-R1.7 failed the classical floor (best
  delta vs MA@5 = -0.55; R1.7's rich-state book +0.58 vs MA +1.69).
- Intraday: SR-type rewards traded the TRAIN-window patterns and lost
  OOS on every venue (NIFTY 50 -1.12/-2.53-style; the only positive fold
  in all of Phase 2 was gold 2026-07 at +1.28, which broke the next
  month).
- No result survives a regime change. Checked and NOT robust -> per KB
  8.7, this alone blocks deployment.

## Q2. Does the agent exploit a friction that will vanish?

**Answer: N/A in the positive direction — no agent found any exploitable
friction at all; the question is moot.**

- The market-structure discovery (R2.1) is the opposite of a vanishing
  friction: NIFTY-family cash-index intraday is NEGATIVE-carry in 2020+
  (intraday-long SR -1.33 NIFTY 50, -0.55 BANK; the daily return is
  carried by overnight gaps). There is no intraday friction to exploit —
  the session bleeds.
- MCX gold (futures and the MCX*DEX index) is zero-drift intraday: no
  friction, no edge.
- Per-bar continuous repositioning at 5bps churned every policy to ruin
  (random -118..-198 Sharpe; PPO -89/-48) — costs are the dominant
  "friction" and they are real, not illusory.
- Nothing to haircut for market adaptation because nothing was found.

## Verdict

Both KB 8.7 questions: NOT passable. The program is a rigorous,
reproducible NEGATIVE: across reward functions (PnL, DSR, U-family,
Sharpe-type, vol-conditioned), costs (5-25bps), state designs (returns,

out-of-sample window. The one reproducible learned behavior is
DRAWDOWN AVOIDANCE (R1.7: MaxDD 8.9% vs MA 15.7% / B&H 25.3%), which
under-earns.

conditions that defeated RL change.

  Gate-B overlay) — live as today.

  drawdown-avoider (MaxDD 8.9%). Paper-only, 5bps costs, monthly review.
- **Frequency**: weekly mark-to-model; monthly review meeting.
- **Success threshold for promotion**: 24 consecutive months where the
  RL book's risk-adjusted return (Sharpe) exceeds the classical book's
  AND the MaxDD advantage persists at <=10bps costs. Absent that, the
  decision stands: no deployment.
- **Revisit triggers**: (a) MCX index history accumulates >2y (the
  plan's mandatory augmentation + a longer intraday sample);

  intraday carry; (c) a fundamental state upgrade (sector-relative,
  order-flow) that the classical rules cannot express.
