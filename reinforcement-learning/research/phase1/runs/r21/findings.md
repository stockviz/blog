## R2.1 synthesis (intraday verdict + market-structure discovery)

1. **The overnight-dominance discovery (the headline)**: intraday long
   (buy first bar, sell last bar, same cost mechanics) on NIFTY 50 in
   2020+ is a LOSING trade — SR -1.33, CAGR -14.8%, MaxDD 63.8%. The
   index's +15.4% daily CAGR is carried entirely by overnight gaps; the
   intraday session itself has negative drift in this window. Any
   intraday-long strategy on NIFTY 50 fights the overnight carry. This is
   the single most important fact for Phase 2.
2. **The Si agent learned FLAT**: with U = mean(DR) - std(DR) and 5bps
   inside DR, inaction is optimal (flat gives U = 0, which beats every
   noisy strategy). The agent ≈ flat with a tiny negative drift from
   occasional flips (SR -0.86, MaxDD 0.1%); the U trace was flat
   throughout training. The honest comparison is vs FLAT (0.0), which
   beats the agent; beating the catastrophic intraday B&H (-1.33) and
   random (-198.8) baselines is vacuous.
3. **Walk-forward**: all 7 post-2020 folds have negative per-day Sharpe —
   no regime where the policy adds value.
4. **R2.1 gate FAILS** (per-day Sharpe -0.86 < 1; zero positive folds).
   Root causes: (a) negative intraday carry on the venue; (b) the U
   objective's flat-optimality; (c) per-bar continuous repositioning at
   5bps is lethal (random = -198 Sharpe).
5. **Implications for R2.2/R2.3**: (a) reward redesign — U needs an
   earnings push (e.g., alpha*mean with alpha > beta, or a Sharpe-type
   ratio over DR) and/or an explicit inactivity penalty so flat is not
   trivially optimal; (b) venue matters — NIFTY BANK (higher intraday
   vol/edge), NIFTY futures (basis/carry), or MCX gold (overnight-less
   global session) are better tests of intraday RL than the cash index;
   (c) the first-bar overnight gap is a real feature to include; (d) cap
   per-bar position change (the S6 paper's continuous action churns at
   5bps).
