# Level 1

Orientation and prerequisites: accessible introductions, investing vocabulary, and the mindset needed to read the rest of the material.

## define: bitcoin

This article introduces Bitcoin as a decentralized, fixed-supply, infinitely divisible digital currency, contrasting it with historical examples of currency debasement and traditional stores of value. It explains basic concepts like blockchain, mining, and the 21 million coin cap, while also discussing trade-offs such as slow transactions and high volatility. The piece positions Bitcoin more as a digital collectible than a practical medium of exchange, making it suitable for readers with no prior knowledge of cryptocurrencies.

## define: ethereum

This article introduces Ethereum as a decentralized platform enabling programmers to build and run distributed applications using smart contracts. It traces Ethereum's origins to Vitalik Buterin's vision of a world computer, contextualizing it within the evolution of virtual worlds and MMORPGs like Second Life. The piece explains foundational concepts including blockchain technology, Ether (ETH) as the platform's native currency, and non-fungible tokens (NFTs) through the CryptoKitties example. It further explores decentralized finance (DeFi), describing how Ethereum enables alternatives to traditional financial services like lending, trading, and insurance without centralized intermediaries. The article frames Ethereum as the largest cryptocurrency ecosystem after Bitcoin, emphasizing its role in constructing self-contained virtual economies. Written in an accessible, narrative-driven style, it serves as an orientation piece for readers new to blockchain technology and digital assets, establishing prerequisite vocabulary for deeper exploration.

## Getting Started

This article serves as an introductory guide for new investors, outlining a simple path to start investing. It recommends beginners to first go through educational resources like Zerodha Varsity to understand basic concepts such as savings, investments, and market regulation. It emphasizes the importance of diversification and the risk-return tradeoff. The article then suggests that the best way to learn is by doing, and promotes the broker's own momentum strategies for portfolios between Rs. 15-30 lakhs, with no additional fees. For larger accounts, customized solutions like risk-barbells are offered. The linked materials include beginner modules on stock markets, taxation, and personal finance, as well as an article on sequence risk and asset allocation, which is more advanced. Overall, the content is oriented towards absolute beginners, providing orientation and prerequisite vocabulary.
