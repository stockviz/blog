## Summary

This document investigates the common market myth that technology stocks rise when the rupee falls against the US dollar. By analyzing scatter plots of daily and weekly returns between USDINR and the CNX IT index, as well as cross-correlation plots with various lags, the analysis demonstrates that no obvious link or predictable correlation exists between currency movements and the performance of technology stocks. The author concludes that currency fluctuations alone cannot serve as a reliable explanation for tech stock movements, debunking journalistic narratives often cited during market events.

## Article

tl;dr No. The Myth Regular viewers of CNBC might have heard the refrain that “IT stocks went up because the rupee went down.” But it turns out that it is the classic case of the journalist/reporter/anchor going in search of a reason to explain a random market event. If there is such a correlation, then a scatter plot of USDINR returns vs. CNX IT index returns should unearth it.
