## Summary

This article questions whether monthly return patterns of the Nifty index are meaningful for investors. It argues that searching for patterns in monthly returns can be misleading because returns fluctuate widely without clear predictive value. Using a broad recap of global equities, commodities, currencies, and Indian sector data, the piece emphasizes that short-term monthly movements often reflect noise rather than signal. It reinforces the idea that quality businesses bought at reasonable prices matter more than timing based on monthly performance snapshots, aligning with Buffett-style investment thinking about price versus value.
