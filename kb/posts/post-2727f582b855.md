## Summary

This article tests a sector momentum rotation strategy using SPDR sector ETFs. It calculates rolling returns over 1, 3, 6, and 12 months, then goes long the best-performing sector for the next month, with a cash alternative if returns are negative. The strategy is backtested on two periods: before 2010 and after 2011. While the strategy outperformed the S&P 500 in the earlier period, it significantly underperformed in the later period, illustrating the dangers of overfitting and the unpredictability of momentum strategies. The article assumes familiarity with ETFs, momentum, and basic backtesting, but does not require advanced statistical knowledge.
