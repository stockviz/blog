## Summary

This document provides an update on institutional investing trends involving Foreign Institutional Investors and Domestic Institutional Investors in September. FIIs invested more heavily in debt than in equities, while DIIs showed a strong preference for debt investments compared to equities. The article highlights the differing perspectives and benefits for investors choosing between debt and equity markets, noting that debt investors stand to gain from a falling interest rate environment and shrinking defaults, whereas equity investors benefit from improving balance sheets and a stronger demand-side equation.

## Article

A quick update to our II Trend report from last month. Foreign Institutional Investors keep the faith FII invested $1742.07 million in debt vs $1221.89 million in equities so far in Septmeber. Domestic Institutionals were obsessed with debt In September, DIIs invested Rs 20,379.6 cr in debt vs. -310.5 cr in equity. A trade for everyone Not sure what I like better – debt or equity. Debt investors will benefit from the falling interest rate environment in AAA paper and shrinking defaults in sub-primes. Equity investors will benefit from improving balance-sheets and an improving demand-side equation. Stay tuned…
