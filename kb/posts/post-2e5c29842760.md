## Summary

This document reviews the performance of 2013 Diwali stock picks from various brokerage houses including Kotak Securities, Microsec, Globe, and Prabhudas Lilladher. It compares their returns and Sharpe ratios against the NIFTY benchmark, which rose 27 percent since the previous Diwali. Kotak Securities yielded the highest return at approximately 50 percent with a Sharpe ratio of 0.16732, while Microsec and Globe both returned about 37 percent with differing volatility patterns. Prabhudas Lilladher achieved a 45 percent return using a concentrated selection of four stocks. Additionally, the article mentions the creation of new themes to track the upcoming 2014 Diwali picks from brokers such as Kotak, Globe, and ET.
