# The SMA Risk On/Off Switch

- Difficulty level: 3
- Published: Sun, 03 Aug 2014 12:12:14 +0000
- Source: [https://stockviz.biz/2014/08/03/sma-risk-onoff-switch/](https://stockviz.biz/2014/08/03/sma-risk-onoff-switch/)

## Summary

This article discusses the application of a simple moving average (SMA) market timing strategy to the Indian Nifty index, based on international research by Meb Faber. The strategy involves going long when the market trades above its 200-day SMA and staying out otherwise, aiming to avoid volatility and negative drawdowns during declining markets. The author examines best and worst market days, cumulative returns, and drawdowns under the SMA switch rule, concluding that utilizing a simple SMA rule helps investors avoid drawdowns and enhance returns compared to a standard buy and hold approach, with smaller look-back periods yielding better performance and lower drawdowns.

## Article

Market timing is a very divisive topic in investing. For traders, it has been the search for the holy grail. For passive investors, a source of derision. Does it mean investors should just remain long all the time? Hardly, according to the latest research by Meb Faber (ssrn). Data for international markets show that volatility increases in declining markets. Any strategy that keeps you out of those periods, will improve your portfolio returns. The outline of his strategy is simple: go long the market if it is trading more than its 200-day SMA and stay out of the market otherwise.
