## Summary

This article provides a comparative performance analysis of the ICICI Prudential Corporate Bond Fund against various peer instruments such as GSecs, the Principal Debt Opportunities Fund, and the UTI Gilt Advantage Fund. Using historical return data and drawdown metrics from specific start dates in 2012 and 2013, the text evaluates the fund's relative strength and risk profile. It concludes that the corporate bond fund serves as an attractive investment option for those seeking exposure to improving corporate credit conditions and potential interest rate cuts by the RBI, while also providing contact details for an AMFI registered independent financial advisor for investment guidance.
