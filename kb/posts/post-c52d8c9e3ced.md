## Summary

This document examines the Turn of the Month effect on equities, referencing a study by John J. McConnell and Wei Xu from the Financial Analysts Journal. Because the original study omitted Indian markets, a quick test was conducted using the CNX 100 index to determine if the turn of the month strategy holds true for Indian equities. The comparison evaluates a Buy-and-Hold strategy against a Turn-of-the-Month strategy. Although the turn of the month strategy demonstrated lower drawdowns, the Buy-and-Hold strategy ultimately won due to better tax advantages and lower trading costs. Consequently, the analysis concludes that the turn of the month effect does not appear to apply to Indian equities.

## Article

The Turn of the Month Effect A recent paper in the Financial Analysts Journal looks at the Turn of the Month effect on equities. Equity Returns at the Turn of the Month, John J. McConnell and Wei Xu: Does it apply to Indian markets? The study skips over the Indian markets. So we did a quick test on the CNX 100 index to check if the effect holds. Here’s the cumulative return chart between a Buy-and-Hold CNX 100 strategy (B&H, black) and a Turn-of-the-Month CNX 100 strategy (TOM, red): Although the TOM strategy has lower-drawdowns, the B&H wins – both in terms of tax advantage and trading costs. The Turn-of-the-Month effect doesn’t seem to apply to Indian equities.
