## Summary

This document provides an applied analysis of stock market performance by evaluating the historical returns of specific Diwali stock picks recommended by major financial publications and brokerage firms like ET and Kotak Securities. It compares the cumulative and annualized returns of these curated thematic portfolios against broader market benchmarks such as the CNX Midcap index over a one-year period. By tracking the actual outcomes of these expert recommendations, the article illustrates practical methods for portfolio backtesting, performance evaluation, and thematic investing. The content requires a working knowledge of stock market indices, portfolio management, and investment performance metrics to fully comprehend the comparative analysis.

## Article

Mostly Paani. Back in October last year, we created two Themes to track the Diwali stock picks collated by ET and Kotak. ET had written that the 10 stocks that they assembled from the recommendations of four brokers could double by next Diwali. However, between 2014-10-21 and 2015-11-02, ET Diwali Picks 2014 returned a cumulative 2.50% (IRR of 2.41%) vs. CNX Midcap’s cumulative return of 17.60% (16.10%.) While Kotak Diwali Picks 2014 has returned a cumulative 14.60% (14.11%.) (ET Picks, Kotak Picks) ET has once again put out stock picks for this Diwali. You can track the portfolio here: Diwali Picks ET 2015 Better luck this year!
