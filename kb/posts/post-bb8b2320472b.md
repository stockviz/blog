## Summary

This article tests an anti-momentum sector rotation strategy using SPDR sector ETFs, buying the worst-performing sector over various look-back periods. It compares performance before and after 2010, finding that while the strategy worked pre-2010, it underperformed the S&P 500 in the last decade. The piece assumes familiarity with ETFs, momentum strategies, and basic backtesting concepts, making it suitable for readers with applied knowledge of investment strategies.
