## Summary

This document explores the application of a 50-day simple moving average tactical theme to the CNX 100 index to protect portfolios from drawdowns and improve cumulative returns compared to a naive buy-and-hold strategy. By utilizing risk-on and risk-off signals, the strategy dynamically allocates investments between NIFTYBEES, JUNIORBEES, and LIQUIDBEES ETFs. The analysis demonstrates that avoiding the worst market days enhances performance across multiple historical periods, such as from 2005 to 2010 and from 2010 onward. This applied method bridges market timing theory with practical ETF allocation, providing quantitative evidence of how moving average filters can be implemented effectively in Indian equity markets.
