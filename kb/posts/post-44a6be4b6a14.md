## Summary

This document provides a performance roundup of various StockViz investment themes, broker-recommended stock portfolios, and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) since March 3rd. It defines what an investment theme and strategy are, detailing how mapping specific portfolios helps investors adhere to a disciplined process, avoid behavioral pitfalls, and systematically track profit and loss. The text lists the percentage returns for numerous proprietary StockViz strategies, such as Market Fliers and Magic Formula Investing, alongside performances from external broker picks like Ashwani Gujral and CS Midcap, as well as several ETFs including PSUBNKBEES and BANKBEES. Finally, it encourages readers to open a demat account with StockViz to invest through these structured themes.

## Article

WHAT IS A “THEME””? A StockViz Investment Theme is a portfolio of stocks that follows a particular strategy. It is a convenient way for you to: - stick to a strategy - follow a preset rebalancing schedule - think in terms of your portfolio strategy rather than individual stocks - avoid common behavioral pitfalls - systematically track your P&L and strategy performance WHAT IS AN INVESTMENT STRATEGY? An investment strategy is a specific way of going about the process of investing. It identifies specific variables that define a stock. Variables can be anything: risk, style, sector, balance-sheet items, etc.. We also have themes based on what other brokers have recommended at different points in time. By mapping specific Themes to your account, you ensure that you stay pure to your strategy allocation. And that there is no “flying by the seat of your pants” investing.
