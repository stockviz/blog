# Re-factored Indices and Momentum

- Difficulty level: 3
- Published: Sat, 06 Dec 2014 07:05:25 +0000
- Source: [https://stockviz.biz/2014/12/06/re-factored-indices-momentum/](https://stockviz.biz/2014/12/06/re-factored-indices-momentum/)

## Summary

The article introduces themes created from National Stock Exchange indices, referred to as re-factored indices, featuring free-float capital-weighted and equal-weighted methodologies that allow direct investment without index funds. It highlights long-term outperformance capabilities, noting that multinational corporations have historically outperformed public sector enterprises while exhibiting lower draw-downs. Additionally, the text discusses short-term investment applications using momentum effects and switching strategies, comparing the momentum of indices such as CNX BANK versus CNX ENERGY, and CNX REALTY versus CNX IT. Ultimately, these re-factored indices serve as ETF-proxies for passive long-term investors and facilitate momentum strategies for short-term traders.

## Article

We have created a whole bunch of Themes based on indices published by the NSE. We call them Re-factored Indices because they use a weighting that is different from NSE’s. We currently have free-float cap weighted and equal weighted themes. Investors can now invest in standard indices directly, without having to go through an index fund. Long-term out performance Some indices show persistent long-term out-performance. For example, MNCs have beaten PSEs (StockViz) and have shown to have lower draw-downs. Investors can map the MNC Theme and let NSE/IISL figure out what stocks should be part of that index. Momentum Short-term investors can use momentum effects to their advantage and implement a switching strategy. For example, CNX BANK shows greater momentum than CNX ENERGY: And over the last week, momentum in CNX REALTY has improved drastically whereas CNX IT has decreased: Conclusion Re-factored Indices can act as ETF-proxies for long-term, passive investors and opens up interesting momentum strategies for short-term investors.
