## Summary

This article analyzes the historical performance of three NSE financial indices in India: CNX PSU BANK, CNX FINANCE, and CNX BANK. By examining cumulative returns since January 2004, the text highlights the lower performance and persistent drawdowns of Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) banks compared to broader financial and banking indices. It discusses investor behavior surrounding PSU banks in the context of political developments like the Modi effect and raises questions about whether future trends will differ from past market cycles. The document combines financial index analysis with market sentiment observation to evaluate sector-specific investment outcomes.
