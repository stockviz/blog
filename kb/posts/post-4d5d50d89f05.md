## Summary

This document examines the low trading volumes and liquidity issues associated with Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) in the Indian financial market, using the NIFTYBEES ETF as a primary example compared to individual stocks like RELIANCE. It analyzes the impact of the National Stock Exchange of India's Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES), which was implemented to incentivize market making and improve ETF liquidity through various extensions until mid-2015. The article highlights that despite these programmatic interventions by the exchange, the results were largely mixed, with some ETFs even experiencing a decline in daily trading volumes. It concludes that while market making programs might help narrow bid-ask spreads, they failed to generate a significant surge in trading volume or overcome the ongoing disinterest among retail investors toward ETF products within the region.
