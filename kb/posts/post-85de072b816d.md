## Summary

This document discusses the analysis of mutual fund performance using a relative strength spread or RS-Spread metric to evaluate outperformance against the CNX 500 benchmark over a one-year period. It explores the persistence and fluctuation of alpha across various top-performing funds and fund houses such as HDFC, ICICI Prudential, Birla Sun Life, Reliance, and UTI. The text highlights that mutual funds within the same asset class tend to revert to the mean over time, meaning consistent outperformance is rare and difficult to sustain. Consequently, investors are often better served by identifying and avoiding underperforming funds and fund houses rather than simply chasing recent top-10 lists. The article also introduces tools like FundCompare to visualize and track historical alpha trends for over a hundred different mutual funds daily.
