## Summary

The document provides a comprehensive review of the financial markets in the year 2015, detailing the performance of various asset classes including commodities, energy, metals, and agricultural products, which mostly experienced declines. Additionally, it outlines bond index movements and changes in yield-to-maturity across different maturities. The text also touches upon mutual fund trends, noting record retail inflows alongside foreign investor outflows, despite global and domestic economic worries. Overall, the article serves as an applied market analysis summarizing returns and investor behavior for the year 2015.
