# Momentum Rank & Portfolio Persistence by Market Regime

**Period:** 2014-07-31 → 2026-06-30  
**Momentum definition:** 12-month lookback, no skip, top 60% FF-mcap universe  
**Portfolios:** Momentum = top 20 by 12-mo return; Mom+Skew = top decile momentum → top tercile expected skewness → top 20  
**Persistence metrics:**  
  1. **Spearman ρ:** Corr(MOM_t, MOM_{t+1}) — score-level rank persistence  
  2. **Mom Overlap:** fraction of Momentum top-20 stocks retained from t → t+1  
  3. **Skew Overlap:** fraction of Mom+Skew top-20 stocks retained from t → t+1  

**Regime definitions (NIFTY500 MOMENTUM 50 TR drawdown from running peak):**

- **Normal:** drawdown > -5%
- **Drawdown:** drawdown ≤ -10%
- **Recovery:** drawdown between -10% and -5%, with positive monthly return
